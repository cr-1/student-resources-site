    Dedicated to my students

    These resources have been selected to provoke curiosity and exploration, provide useful knowledge, and expose you to different ideas and perspectives. Inclusion does not mean that every claim or argument in a book is endorsed. Books are different kinds of sources: scientific research, popular science, journalism, history, memoir, criticism, practical guides, and works of argument. Read accordingly. Ask what evidence supports a claim, what perspective the author brings, what may have changed since publication, and what credible alternative interpretations exist.

    Remember: you can take a stand while remaining open to changing your mind as you learn more. Science advances by revising what we think we know. So can you.

    Explore

    These resources have been selected as starting points for exploration. You are not expected to read or watch everything. Follow your interests, compare different sources and question what you encounter.

    Contributions

    Students and collaborators are welcome to suggest additions. Before recommending a resource, please consider: * Is it accurate and trustworthy? * Is it appropriate for students? * Have you read or reviewed it? * Is the link legal and reliable? * Does it add something useful to the collection?
