    About this collection

    Welcome! This repository is an evolving curated collection of books, articles, papers, videos, courses, websites, you name it! It offers selected starting points for exploring new ideas. Curiosity, critical thinking and independent learning are essential skills for every student. I hope these support you in your quests, lead you somewhere unexpected, and allow you to have fun along the way.

    Resources are organised by topic and, whenever possible, include: - a short description; - why the resource is valuable; - the expected level of difficulty; - recommended prerequisites.

    This repository intends to be a living collection maintained by the community. Everyone is encouraged to contribute by suggesting new resources, improving descriptions or correcting outdated information.

    Guiding principles

    • Be curious;
    • Think critically;
    • Verify your sources;
    • Learn continuously;
    • Share what you discover.

    Code of conduct

    This repository is part of an extensive learning community. Everyone should feel welcome to learn, ask questions and share knowledge. If anything triggers you, feel free to reach out to me.

    Expectations

    You are encouraged to: - Treat others with respect. - Be curious and open-minded. - Support claims with reliable evidence. - Give constructive feedback. - Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them. - Credit the work of others.

    Unacceptable behaviour

    This includes, but is not limited to: - Harassment or discrimination; - Personal attacks; - Plagiarism; - Deliberate misinformation; - Disruptive or disrespectful behaviour.

    Philosophy

    Learning is collaborative. Nobody knows everything, and everyone has something to contribute. - Be curious; - Be brave; - Be kind; - Keep learning.

    Repository structure

    The collection is organised by type of resource, with topics and themes connecting materials across sections. It will evolve into something like: - Readings - Fiction - Non-Fiction - In the Media - Scientific Papers - Courses - Online resources - Videos - Podcasts - Projects & Activities

    Happy exploring!
