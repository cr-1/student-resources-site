    Non-fiction

    An evolving collection, with selected chapters or extracts connected to activities. The groupings indicate a starting point; several books are relevant across disciplines. Some titles are available to read legally online at no cost. These are marked 🆓

    Critical Thinking & Science

    How Life Works — Philip Ball

    An exploration of contemporary biology that challenges simplified explanations of genes, organisms, and biological systems.

    #biology #science #systems-thinking #genetics #complexity

    Surely you’re joking Mr Feynman - Ralph Leighton, Richard Feynman 🆓

    A collection of humorous and insightful autobiographical stories from Nobel Prize-winning physicist Richard Feynman. celebrating curiosity, independent thinking, and the joy of learning.

    #scientific-method #critical-thinking #problem-solving #curiosity #physics #recommended

    Bad Science — Ben Goldacre 🆓

    An accessible examination of scientific evidence, misleading claims, statistics and the way science is communicated in the media. Learn how to evaluate scientific claims, understand statistics, and recognise common mistakes in science communication.

    #critical-thinking #scientific-literacy #statistics #recommended

    The Demon-Haunted World. Science as a Candle in the Dark — Carl Sagan 🆓

    An exploration of scientific thinking, skepticism, and the importance of evidence in distinguishing reliable knowledge from pseudoscience, superstition, and misinformation. Sagan argues for science not only as a body of knowledge, but as a way of questioning claims, recognising uncertainty, and thinking critically about the world.

    #scientific-literacy #critical-thinking #scientific-method #pseudoscience #misinformation #recommended

    Psychology, Behaviour & Decision Making

    Thinking, Fast and Slow — Daniel Kahneman

    An influential exploration of judgment and decision making, contrasting fast, intuitive responses with slower, more deliberate reasoning and examining the biases that can affect both. Note: some studies discussed in the book, particularly research on social priming, were later challenged during psychology's replication crisis. A useful opportunity to explore how scientific knowledge changes. See Kahneman's 2012 open letter on social priming.

    #psychology #decision-making #cognitive-bias #reasoning #behaviour #critical-thinking

    Not Born Yesterday — Hugo Mercier

    An exploration of why humans are not as gullible as commonly assumed, examining how we evaluate information, trust others, and decide what to believe.

    #critical-thinking #psychology #trust #misinformation #reasoning #communication

    Free Agents — Kevin J. Mitchell

    An exploration of agency and free will through neuroscience, evolution, and biology, arguing that living organisms have evolved increasingly sophisticated capacities to make choices and act on their own reasons.

    #neuroscience #biology #free-will #agency #decision-making #behaviour #evolution #recommended

    Noise — Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony & Cass R. Sunstein

    An examination of unwanted variability in human judgment, exploring why equally qualified people can reach very different decisions when considering the same evidence.

    #decision-making #judgment #bias #psychology #statistics #organisations

    Determined — Robert M. Sapolsky

    An exploration of human behaviour through biology, neuroscience, psychology, and environment, questioning conventional ideas about free will and individual responsibility.

    #neuroscience #psychology #behaviour #biology #free-will #ethics

    Look Again — Tali Sharot & Cass R. Sunstein

    An exploration of habituation and how becoming accustomed to our surroundings can make us overlook both problems and sources of pleasure, asking how changing perspective can help us notice them again.

    #psychology #habituation #attention #perception #behaviour #perspective

    Design & Making

    The Design of Everyday Things — Don Norman

    An influential introduction to human-centred design, exploring how objects and systems communicate how they should be used and why poor design is often mistaken for user error.

    #design #human-centred-design #usability #hci #psychology #problem-solving #recommended

    Photography & Visual Culture

    Photography & Society — Gisèle Freund

    A social and historical examination of photography, exploring how photographic practices developed alongside changing technologies, social classes, political institutions, journalism, and mass culture.

    #photography #visual-culture #history #society #media #representation

    A Short History of Photography — Walter Benjamin

    An influential essay on the early history of photography, considering portraiture, technological change, reproduction, artistic value, and the changing ways photographs are made and encountered.

    #photography #visual-culture #history #art #technology #critical-thinking

    The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction — Walter Benjamin

    An influential essay examining how technologies of reproduction such as photography and film transform the status, circulation, experience, and political possibilities of art.

    #photography #art #reproduction #technology #media #visual-culture #critical-thinking

    Camera Lucida — Roland Barthes

    A personal and philosophical meditation on photography, memory, loss, attention, and the distinctive relationship between a photograph and the reality it records.

    #photography #visual-culture #memory #representation #philosophy #art

    On Photography — Susan Sontag

    A collection of essays examining photography as an artistic, social, and political practice, asking how photographs shape the ways we look at events, other people, suffering, and the world around us.

    #photography #visual-culture #representation #ethics #media #society #critical-thinking #recommended

    Ways of Seeing — John Berger

    An accessible and influential exploration of how we look at images, examining art, photography, advertising, gender, ownership, and the ways social and cultural contexts shape what we see.

    #visual-culture #photography #art #advertising #representation #media-literacy #critical-thinking #recommended

    Another Way of Telling — John Berger & Jean Mohr

    An exploration of how photographs create meaning and tell stories, combining photographic sequences and essays to investigate memory, narrative, context, and the relationship between images and words.

    #photography #storytelling #visual-narrative #memory #representation #images-and-text #recommended

    Towards a Philosophy of Photography — Vilém Flusser

    A philosophical examination of photography and photographic technology, asking how cameras shape the possibilities available to photographers and how technical images influence the ways we understand the world.

    #photography #philosophy #technology #visual-culture #media #critical-thinking

    The Civil Contract of Photography — Ariella Aïsha Azoulay

    A theoretical exploration of photography as a relationship between photographer, photographed person, and viewer, examining citizenship, power, violence, responsibility, and the politics of looking.

    #photography #ethics #politics #representation #citizenship #power #visual-culture

    Listening to Images — Tina M. Campt

    An exploration of archival photographs of Black communities and the quieter forms of expression, resistance, and possibility that can emerge when images are examined beyond their most immediately visible meanings.

    #photography #archives #race #representation #history #visual-culture #critical-thinking

    Black Looks: Race and Representation — bell hooks

    A collection of essays examining race, gender, representation, spectatorship, and the politics of looking across photography, film, television, and popular culture.

    #visual-culture #race #gender #representation #media #critical-thinking

    Blind Spot — Teju Cole

    A combination of photographs and short texts that explores travel, observation, memory, politics, coincidence, and the connections that emerge through attentive looking.

    #photography #writing #observation #visual-storytelling #memory #place #art

    The Nature of Photographs — Stephen Shore

    A concise introduction to understanding how photographs work as visual objects, examining framing, focus, time, viewpoint, composition, and the choices through which photographers transform the world into an image.

    #photography #composition #visual-literacy #observation #art #practice

    The Photographer's Playbook: 307 Assignments and Ideas — Jason Fulford & Gregory Halpern (eds.)

    A collection of assignments, exercises, ideas, and provocations from photographers and educators, designed to encourage experimentation, observation, creative risk-taking, and the development of an individual photographic practice.

    #photography #practice #exercises #creativity #experimentation #observation #assignments #recommended

    On Photographers

    Diane Arbus: An Aperture Monograph — Diane Arbus

    An influential collection of Arbus's portraits, bringing together photographs of people, relationships, identities, and social worlds through a distinctive and sometimes unsettling approach to portraiture and observation.

    #photography #portraiture #identity #representation #observation #society #visual-culture

    Photographs of Famous Men & Fair Women — Julia Margaret Cameron

    A collection of portraits and staged photographs by one of the most distinctive photographers of the nineteenth century, offering a way to explore portraiture, expression, staging, focus, literary influence, and the emergence of photography as an artistic medium.

    #photography #portraiture #history-of-photography #staging #women-photographers #visual-culture #art``#recommended

    Raised by Wolves — Jim Goldberg

    A photographic and documentary work about young people living on the streets in California, combining photographs with handwritten texts, interviews, drawings, found materials, and other forms of testimony to construct a complex narrative between documentation and storytelling.

    #photography #documentary #photobook #storytelling #text-and-image #youth #representation #recommended

    The Ballad of Sexual Dependency — Nan Goldin

    An intensely personal photographic account of relationships, intimacy, friendship, sexuality, nightlife, love, conflict, and loss, constructed from photographs of Goldin herself and the communities around her.

    #photography #photobook #intimacy #relationships #identity #community #diary #visual-narrative #recommended

    The Last Resort — Martin Parr

    A vivid and often humorous photographic portrait of leisure and everyday life in the British seaside resort of New Brighton, using colour, observation, and visual irony to examine class, consumption, family life, and the ordinary.

    #photography #documentary #colour #observation #everyday-life #consumer-culture #society #humour #recommended

    Utterly Lazy and Inattentive — Martin Parr

    A retrospective selection of photographs chosen by Parr from across his career, bringing together decades of observation of everyday life, leisure, consumption, tourism, class, behaviour, and the absurdities of the ordinary.

    #photography #documentary #observation #everyday-life #consumer-culture #humour #society #colour #recommended

    The Complete Untitled Film Stills — Cindy Sherman

    A series of staged self-portraits in which Sherman performs fictional female characters reminiscent of film imagery, using photography to explore identity, gender, representation, stereotypes, and the construction of images.

    #photography #self-portraiture #staging #gender #identity #representation #film #conceptual-art #recommended

    An American Exodus: A Record of Human Erosion — Dorothea Lange & Paul S. Taylor

    A documentary study of migration, poverty, labour, and displacement during the Great Depression, combining Lange's photographs with Taylor's research, field notes, captions, and the words of the people they encountered.

    #photography #documentary #migration #labour #poverty #history #text-and-image #social-documentary

    Storytelling & Creative Practice

    Narration in the Fiction Film — David Bordwell

    An examination of how films communicate narrative information and how viewers construct stories through visual cues, editing, time, perspective, and cinematic conventions.

    #film #narratology #cinema #visual-storytelling #cognition #editing #critical-thinking #recommended

    Actual Minds, Possible Worlds — Jerome Bruner

    An exploration of different ways of constructing knowledge and meaning, examining the role of narrative in human thought, imagination, interpretation, and our understanding of experience.

    #psychology #narrative #cognition #storytelling #meaning #imagination #recommended

    The Hero with a Thousand Faces — Joseph Campbell

    An influential theory of recurring patterns in mythology and storytelling across different traditions. Campbell's idea of the 'monomyth' has had a major influence on modern storytelling, although its claim to universality has been challenged by scholars of mythology and folklore

    #mythology #storytelling #narrative #culture

    Story and Discourse: Narrative Structure in Fiction and Film — Seymour Chatman

    An exploration of narrative structure across literature and cinema, examining the distinction between story and discourse, narrative time, character, perspective, and the different ways stories are communicated through words and images.

    #narratology #storytelling #literature #film #narrative-structure #representation #recommended

    Steering the Craft — Ursula K. Le Guin

    A practical exploration of the craft of writing, focusing on language, rhythm, point of view, sentence construction, and narrative technique.

    #writing #storytelling #creativity #craft

    On Writing — Stephen King

    Part memoir and part practical guide to writing, reflecting on reading, discipline, revision, language, and the development of a writing practice.

    #writing #storytelling #creativity #craft #recommended

    Understanding Comics: The Invisible Art — Scott McCloud 🆓

    An accessible visual exploration of how sequential images communicate stories and ideas, examining framing, time, transitions, symbols, perception, and the relationship between images and words.

    #visual-storytelling #comics #narrative #art #design #visual-literacy #creativity #recommended

    Orality and Literacy — Walter J. Ong

    An influential examination of how oral and written forms of communication shape memory, expression, storytelling, and cultural practices, exploring the relationship between communication technologies and human thought.

    #storytelling #oral-tradition #literacy #communication #media #culture #history

    Morphology of the Folktale — Vladimir Propp

    A structural analysis of Russian folktales identifying recurring narrative functions and character roles, providing a foundational approach to studying patterns in storytelling.

    #narratology #folklore #structuralism #storytelling #narrative-structure #literature #classic

    The Science of Storytelling — Will Storr

    Here is one attempt to connect what we know about cognition with storytelling practice. It presents an exploration of storytelling through psychology and neuroscience, examining how character, change, curiosity, and models of the world shape narratives.

    #storytelling #psychology #narrative #creativity

    Aristotle's Poetics for Screenwriters — Michael Tierno

    A practical interpretation of Aristotle's ideas about drama, adapted to the construction and analysis of contemporary screen stories. #storytelling #screenwriting #narrative #film #recommended

    The Anatomy of Story — John Truby 🆓

    A practical framework for developing stories through character, motivation, moral choices, conflict, and interconnected narrative elements, offering an alternative to rigid act-based storytelling formulas.

    #storytelling #screenwriting #character-development #narrative-structure #writing #creative-practice #recommended

    The Writer's Journey — Christopher Vogler

    A practical story-design framework for storytelling based on recurring narrative patterns and the concept of the hero's journey.

    #storytelling #narrative #screenwriting #structure

    Creativity, Inc. — Ed Catmull, with Amy Wallace

    A reflection on creativity, collaboration, leadership, and organisational culture based on Catmull's experience building and leading Pixar.

    #creativity #collaboration #leadership #organisations #creative-practice

    Writing, Communication & Media

    On Writing Well — William Zinsser

    A practical guide to writing clear, concise, engaging non-fiction, with an emphasis on simplicity, revision, and respect for the reader.

    #writing #communication #clarity #non-fiction

    Everybody Writes — Ann Handley

    A practical guide to writing clearly and effectively for digital communication, from longer texts to everyday online content.

    #writing #communication #digital-media #content

    Free Culture - Lawrence Lessig 🆓

    An introduction to copyright, creativity, the public domain, and the impact of intellectual property law on innovation and culture.

    #copyright #creative-commons #open-knowledge #digital-culture #intellectual-property #recommended

    Amusing Ourselves to Death — Neil Postman 🆓

    A thought-provoking examination of how entertainment and mass media influence public discourse, education, and democratic life. Although written in 1985, many of its ideas resonate strongly in the age of social media.

    #critical-thinking #media-literacy #education #communication #society #recommended

    Technology, AI & Society

    The New Empire of AI — Rachel Adams

    An examination of artificial intelligence through global inequality, exploring how AI systems depend on existing international distributions of labour, resources, wealth, and political power.

    #ai #global-inequality #technology #power #labour #global-south #society

    On Internet Freedom — Marvin Ammori

    An exploration of internet policy, freedom of expression, innovation, and the legal and political questions surrounding an open internet.

    #internet #digital-rights #law #freedom-of-expression #technology #recommended

    Race After Technology — Ruha Benjamin

    An examination of how technologies that appear neutral can reproduce and deepen racial inequality, introducing ways of thinking critically about the relationship between design, automation, classification, and discrimination.

    #technology #race #algorithms #bias #design #inequality #society

    Dark Matters — Simone Browne

    An exploration of surveillance through the history and experience of Black communities, connecting contemporary technologies such as biometrics and security systems with longer histories of racialised surveillance.

    #surveillance #race #history #technology #biometrics #privacy #society

    Artificial Unintelligence — Meredith Broussard

    A critique of the assumption that computers can or should solve every problem, exploring the limitations of computational systems and the consequences of treating technological solutions as inherently superior.

    #ai #technology #algorithms #critical-thinking #technochauvinism #society

    More Than a Glitch — Meredith Broussard

    An examination of how racism, sexism, and ableism can become embedded in technological systems, arguing that discriminatory outcomes should not simply be treated as isolated technical errors.

    #ai #algorithms #bias #race #gender #disability #inequality #technology

    Unmasking AI — Joy Buolamwini

    A personal and research-informed account of uncovering bias in facial-analysis technologies and the wider effort to challenge discrimination and exclusion in artificial intelligence.

    #ai #facial-recognition #algorithms #bias #race #gender #accountability

    Brotopia — Emily Chang

    An investigation into gender inequality and workplace culture in Silicon Valley, examining how the technology industry's institutions, networks, and working environments have shaped who participates in building technology and who holds power within the industry.

    #technology #silicon-valley #gender #women-in-technology #workplace-culture #inequality #EDI

    Atlas of AI — Kate Crawford

    An examination of artificial intelligence as a material and political system, considering data, labour, natural resources, classification, and power alongside the technology itself.

    #ai #technology #data #labour #power #ethics #society #recommended

    The AI Con — Emily M. Bender & Alex Hanna

    A critical examination of claims made about artificial intelligence, asking what current AI systems actually do, who benefits from exaggerated expectations about them, and what social and material costs can be obscured by the language surrounding AI. It's a deliberately sceptical examination of contemporary AI claims and industry narratives. Best read alongside technically grounded accounts offering different assessments of AI capabilities and limitations.

    #ai #critical-thinking #technology #language-models #industry #labour #power

    Empire of AI — Karen Hao

    A reported investigation into the development of the contemporary AI industry, examining its institutions, ambitions, labour, environmental resources, and concentrations of power through the history and expansion of OpenAI.

    #ai #technology #labour #environment #power #industry #society

    Your Face Belongs to Us — Kashmir Hill

    An investigation into the development of Clearview AI and the rapid expansion of facial-recognition technology, examining privacy, surveillance, data collection, law enforcement, and the consequences of making faces searchable at scale.

    #facial-recognition #privacy #surveillance #ai #biometrics #data #digital-rights

    AI Snake Oil — Arvind Narayanan & Sayash Kapoor

    An evidence-based examination of what artificial intelligence can and cannot reliably do, distinguishing genuine advances from exaggerated claims and investigating areas such as predictive algorithms, generative AI, automated decision-making, and the AI industry.

    #ai #critical-thinking #evidence #algorithms #prediction #generative-ai #technology

    Silicon Valley Imperialism — Erin McElroy

    An examination of how Silicon Valley's models of technology, investment, property, and development travel across borders, connecting the San Francisco Bay Area with postsocialist Romania through questions of displacement, inequality, and technological power.

    #technology #silicon-valley #globalisation #inequality #housing #political-economy #europe

    Blood in the Machine — Brian Merchant

    A history of the Luddites and their resistance to the ways industrial technologies were being used to transform labour, wages, skills, and power during the Industrial Revolution, connecting their experience with contemporary debates about automation.

    #technology #labour #history #automation #industrial-revolution #workers #society

    Dying for an iPhone — Jenny Chan, Mark Selden & Pun Ngai

    An investigation into the human labour behind global electronics manufacturing, examining factory conditions, worker experiences, supply chains, and the social costs embedded in consumer technology.

    #technology #labour #china #supply-chains #manufacturing #globalisation #inequality

    Obfuscation — Finn Brunton & Helen Nissenbaum

    An exploration of techniques for resisting pervasive data collection by deliberately producing ambiguous or misleading information, combining practical examples with questions about privacy, power, protest, and the ethics of resisting surveillance.

    #privacy #surveillance #data #digital-rights #resistance #ethics #technology

    Algorithms of Oppression — Safiya Umoja Noble

    An examination of how search engines can reproduce and amplify racism and sexism, challenging the assumption that information technologies and their results are inherently neutral.

    #algorithms #search-engines #race #gender #bias #technology #inequality

    Weapons of Math Destruction — Cathy O'Neil

    An accessible examination of how mathematical models and algorithmic decision systems can reproduce inequality when they are opaque, difficult to challenge, and applied at scale in areas such as education, employment, credit, and criminal justice.

    #algorithms #data #bias #inequality #accountability #critical-thinking #recommended

    Against Tech Oligarchy — J. S. Tan & Clarissa Redwine

    An account of worker organising within the technology industry, examining employee protests, unionisation, workplace conflict, corporate power, and changing relationships between technology workers and the companies they help build.

    #technology #labour #workers #unions #silicon-valley #corporate-power #society

    The Mechanic and the Luddite — Jathan Sadowski
