    Fiction

    Reading selection to encourage curiosity, imagination, empathy, critical thinking, and exploration of different perspectives, cultures, and possible worlds. (order: author's surname)

    Things Fall Apart — Chinua Achebe

    A novel about Igbo society and the disruptive arrival of British colonial rule and Christian missionaries in what is now Nigeria. #colonialism #nigeria #culture #identity #history #african-literature #classic

    Americanah — Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie

    A novel about migration, race, identity, relationships, and belonging, viewed through the experiences of a Nigerian woman living between Nigeria and the United States. #migration #race #identity #belonging #nigeria #society

    Fahrenheit 451 — Ray Bradbury

    A novel about censorship, conformity, mass media, and a society increasingly disconnected from books, reflection, and independent thought. #dystopia #censorship #media-literacy #conformity #critical-thinking #classic

    Kindred — Octavia E. Butler

    A contemporary Black woman is repeatedly transported to a slave plantation in nineteenth-century Maryland, confronting the relationship between history, race, power, family, and survival. #historical-fiction #race #slavery #history #power #identity #recommended

    Lilith's Brood (Xenogenesis Trilogy) — Octavia E. Butler

    Dawn · Adulthood Rites · Imago After humanity nearly destroys itself, the surviving humans encounter the Oankali, an alien species offering survival at a profound biological and social cost. Across three novels, Butler explores consent, power, difference, race, gender, hierarchy, genetic engineering, and what it means to remain human while becoming something new. #science-fiction #bioethics #genetics #consent #power #identity #race #gender #humanity #recommended

    Parable of the Sower — Octavia E. Butler

    A young woman navigates a collapsing society shaped by inequality, violence, and environmental crisis while trying to imagine a different future. #speculative-fiction #climate #inequality #community #resilience #society

    The Three-Body Problem — Liu Cixin

    A science-fiction novel connecting fundamental physics, China's Cultural Revolution, technological development, and humanity's response to the possibility of extraterrestrial intelligence. #science-fiction #physics #technology #china #history #first-contact

    The Neverending Story — Michael Ende

    A fantasy novel about a boy who enters the world of a mysterious book, exploring imagination, storytelling, identity, desire, and the relationship between the worlds we create and the lives we live. #fantasy #imagination #storytelling #creativity #identity #coming-of-age #german-literature #recommended

    Cien años de soledad (Cem Anos de Solidão) — Gabriel García Márquez

    A multigenerational story of the Buendía family and the fictional town of Macondo, blending history, myth, politics, memory, and the extraordinary to explore recurring patterns in families and societies. #magical-realism #family #memory #history #latin-america #colombia #society #classic #recommended

    Lessons in Chemistry — Bonnie Garmus

    A novel centred on a female chemist confronting sexism and restrictive social expectations in the 1950s and 1960s, combining science, humour, and questions about equality and opportunity. #science #chemistry #gender-equality #women-in-science #discrimination #society

    A Árvore das Palavras — Teolinda Gersão

    A coming-of-age novel set in colonial Mozambique, exploring childhood, family, race, colonialism, belonging, and the tensions between different cultural worlds. #colonialism #mozambique #race #identity #belonging #history #portuguese-literature

    A Cidade de Ulisses — Teolinda Gersão

    A novel in which Lisbon becomes both setting and subject, intertwining memory, love, art, identity, and the ways people construct stories about places and about themselves. #identity #memory #art #place #lisbon #relationships #portuguese-literature

    Os Teclados — Teolinda Gersão

    A novel about a young girl's encounter with music, family expectations, artistic vocation, and the struggle to create an independent inner life. #music #creativity #identity #family #women #art #portuguese-literature

    Homegoing — Yaa Gyasi

    An intergenerational novel following two branches of a Ghanaian family across centuries, connecting slavery, colonialism, migration, racism, memory, and inherited history. #historical-fiction #ghana #slavery #colonialism #race #family #memory

    The Midnight Library — Matt Haig

    A novel about regret, possibility, alternative lives, and the choices through which people construct meaning and value. #contemporary-fiction #choices #identity #meaning #perspective

    Brave New World — Aldous Huxley

    A dystopian exploration of technological control, consumerism, conformity, pleasure, and the tension between individual freedom and social stability. #dystopia #technology #society #conformity #ethics #classic

    Never Let Me Go — Kazuo Ishiguro

    A quiet and unsettling novel that raises questions about humanity, biotechnology, mortality, social acceptance, and the ethical boundaries of scientific progress. #speculative-fiction #bioethics #technology #humanity #identity #ethics #recommended

    Flowers for Algernon — Daniel Keyes

    A moving exploration of intelligence, disability, scientific experimentation, dignity, relationships, and what we mean when we call someone intelligent. #science-fiction #intelligence #disability #ethics #science #empathy #recommended

    The Left Hand of Darkness — Ursula K. Le Guin

    A landmark science-fiction novel that uses an unfamiliar society to explore gender, identity, culture, politics, and what it means to understand people different from ourselves. #science-fiction #gender #identity #culture #society #recommended

    A Wrinkle in Time — Madeleine L'Engle

    A young science-fiction adventure across space and time that combines mathematics, individuality, courage, family, and resistance to conformity. #science-fiction #mathematics #space #individuality #family #curiosity #classic

    The Memory Police — Yoko Ogawa

    A surreal novel about an island where objects, memories, and eventually parts of people's lives systematically disappear, exploring memory, loss, authoritarianism, and resistance. #speculative-fiction #memory #authoritarianism #identity #loss #resistance

    1984 — George Orwell

    A dystopian novel about totalitarianism, surveillance, propaganda, language, and the manipulation of truth. #dystopia #surveillance #propaganda #language #power #critical-thinking #classic

    Animal Farm — George Orwell

    A political allegory about a group of farm animals whose rebellion against their human owner develops into a new system of oppression, exploring power, propaganda, inequality, and the corruption of revolutionary ideals. #allegory #power #propaganda #inequality #authoritarianism #politics #critical-thinking #classic #recommended

    Frankenstein — Mary Shelley

    A foundational work of science fiction exploring scientific ambition, responsibility, creation, isolation, and the consequences of rejecting what we do not understand. #science-fiction #science #ethics #responsibility #identity #classic #recommended

    Cat's Cradle — Kurt Vonnegut

    A satirical novel about science, technological responsibility, belief, and the unintended consequences of human invention. #science #technology #ethics #responsibility #satire #society #recommended

    Slaughterhouse-Five — Kurt Vonnegut

    A darkly comic and fragmented anti-war novel about memory, trauma, fatalism, and the absurdity of trying to make sense of mass destruction. #war #memory #trauma #absurdism #society #critical-thinking #recommended

    The Martian — Andy Weir

    A survival story built around engineering, scientific reasoning, improvisation, and solving one problem after another under extreme constraints. #science-fiction #engineering #problem-solving #space #science #resilience #recommended

    Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir

    A science-fiction adventure centred on scientific discovery, experimentation, problem-solving, communication, and cooperation in the face of an existential challenge. #science-fiction #science #problem-solving #space #communication #cooperation
